Villa Management in Paphos

A villa isn't a large apartment. It's a building with machinery attached — and the machinery is what fails while you're away.

Get a Free Quote +357 97 950 559

Everything a villa has that a flat doesn't

Pool pump and filtration. Irrigation and outside taps. Multiple A/C units. A roof and terraces with their own drainage. Gates, external lighting, sometimes a borehole or pressure system. Each is a thing that runs unattended for months and each has a failure mode that gets expensive quietly.

That's the real argument for managing a villa properly: not tidiness, but the fact that a €60 part replaced in March is a €2,000 problem in July.

The systems we keep on top of

Villa visits are longer than apartment visits because there's more that can go wrong out of sight.

  • Pool: chemical balance, filter, pump and plant room condition
  • Irrigation: timers, leaks and dead zones before the planting dies
  • Air conditioning: seasonal service, filters and drainage
  • Water: pressure, heaters, tanks and any sign of a slow leak
  • Structure: roof, terraces, balconies and external paintwork

Grounds that reflect the house

A well-kept villa with a neglected garden still reads as neglected — and it's the first thing a guest, a valuer or a prospective buyer registers. We keep lawns, planting, olive trees and hard landscaping in proportion through the year rather than rescuing them before each visit.

Repairs and improvements without flying out

Because repairs, damp work and renovations are done by our own team, an issue found on a Tuesday inspection doesn't need a separate contractor hunt. You get a quote, you say yes or no, and you get photographs when it's finished — the whole loop runs without you being in the country.
