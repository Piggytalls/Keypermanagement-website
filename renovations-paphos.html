Managing a renovation from another country

Renovating remotely is where most overseas owners get burned. Work starts, the scope drifts, communication thins out, and the only way to know what's happening is to fly out and look. By then the money's spent.

We approach it the other way around. A written quote before anything starts, photographs as the work progresses, and a single point of contact who was already visiting your property before this job existed and will still be visiting after it's finished.

The work we take on

Smaller jobs and bigger projects alike — the range is wide because the same team handles your routine maintenance.

  • Bathroom and kitchen refurbishment
  • Tiling, plastering and rendering
  • Interior and exterior painting and decorating
  • Joinery, fitted storage and door and window work
  • Full room renovations and layout changes
  • Making good after damp treatment or water damage

Quoted before we start, photographed when it's done

No work begins without a written quote you've agreed. If something unexpected turns up behind a wall — which on older properties it does — you hear about it and price it before we carry on, not in the final invoice. Progress photographs mean you can follow the job without being here.

Renovating around a letting calendar

If the property earns money, the work has to fit the gaps. We schedule renovation projects into the quiet months and stage them around bookings where we can, so upgrading a bathroom doesn't cost you a season of income.

Upgrades that pay for themselves

Not every improvement is worth doing. The ones that reliably return — better A/C, proper insulation and waterproofing, a bathroom that photographs well, outdoor space that's usable in summer — do so through lower running costs, better bookings or a better sale. We'll say when we think a job isn't worth the money.
