Property Management in Coral Bay

Coral Bay properties work hardest in the months you're least able to be there. Keyper handles the turnovers, the upkeep and the things that go wrong at 9pm on a Saturday.

A resort address means a busier property

Coral Bay is a short-let market. Owners here tend to have guests in and out through the season, which changes what management means — it's less about checking on an empty house and more about a property being genuinely ready, again and again, on a deadline.

It also means wear. Salt air, sand, sun on external paintwork and soft furnishings, and far more use of A/C, plumbing and pool than a private holiday home ever sees.

Changeovers that don't slip

A turnover is a fixed deadline with no flexibility — the next guests arrive whether or not the linen did. Ours run to a checklist and get photographed, so you can see the property was actually ready rather than take our word for it.

  • Full clean between every stay, not a tidy-round
  • Fresh linens and towels, laundered and swapped out
  • Consumables restocked and bins dealt with
  • Damage and wear flagged with photos before the next arrival
  • Welcome pack on request for guests who've travelled all day

Keeping ahead of the season's wear

The point of managing a busy property is to spot the slow decline before a guest does. We track the things that quietly degrade over a season — A/C performance, grout and sealant, pool plant, external paintwork and anything salt has got to — and deal with them in the quiet months when it's cheaper and nobody's staying.

Somebody local when something breaks mid-stay

Guests don't call the owner, they call whoever answers. A failed A/C unit in August or a blocked drain on a changeover day needs a person in Paphos with keys, not an email chain across three time zones. Our maintenance team can usually be there the same day.
