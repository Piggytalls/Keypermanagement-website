Property Management in Tala

Tala sits high enough to get a real winter. Keyper looks after properties here through it — and through the long quiet stretches when nobody's home.

Get a Free Quote +357 97 950 559

Higher ground, cooler summers, wetter winters

Tala's elevation is why people buy here, and it's also why the properties need watching. It's noticeably cooler than the coast in August and noticeably wetter and windier in January, and the second half of that is what puts a house at risk.

Stone and older-build properties in the village hold moisture differently to new coastal apartments. Closed up for months without heating or airflow, they're prime candidates for condensation, musty rooms and damp working its way into plaster.

Winter is the job here

For a Tala property, the months you're least likely to visit are the months that matter most. Our winter routine is built around it.

  • Ventilation and airflow checks so rooms aren't left to sweat
  • Gutters, downpipes and terrace drainage cleared before the rain
  • Early damp and mould spotting on cold external walls
  • Roof and flashing checked after storms, not months later
  • Heating and A/C run periodically so they still work in spring

Grounds and olive trees, season by season

Village plots here often come with mature olive trees and established planting that reward being left alone at the right times and cut back hard at others. We work to the season rather than a fixed fortnightly mow, which usually costs less and always looks better.

Photo reports, because you can't drop in

Every visit produces a report with photographs, sent the same day. It isn't decoration — for an owner two thousand miles away it's the only honest way to know whether the roof really was fine in November, and it's what makes a small repair a decision rather than a surprise.
